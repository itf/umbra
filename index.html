Audio Navigator

Put on headphones. Close your eyes. Walk to the beacon by sound alone. Tap left and right to step — alternate your feet, find a steady rhythm, and don't rush, or you'll stumble. Turn by dragging the upper area (or switch to the compass dial). Tap echo to clap and hear the room.

Keyboard: A = left step, D = right step, ← → = turn (Shift to turn farther), ↓ = probe/echo, ? or H = controls help.
